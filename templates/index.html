USB Connection Setup

💡 Tip: USB connection is recommended for initial setup as it's more reliable.

1

Prepare Your Device

Ensure your Peloton Bike or Tread is powered on and ready. For Peloton Bikes: verify the sensor cable is connected to the tablet.

2

Access Settings Menu

On your device tablet, navigate to Settings. If you're on a Peloton, look for Device Preferences or System Settings.

3

Enable Developer Mode

Find About Device and tap the Build Number field exactly 7 times in quick succession. You'll see a message confirming Developer Options are now enabled.

4

Enable USB Debugging

Go back to Settings and find Developer Options. Enable the USB Debugging toggle.

5

Connect USB Cable

Use a quality USB cable to connect your computer to the device. A micro-USB cable works well for most equipment.

6

Authorize Connection

A prompt will appear on your device asking to authorize USB debugging. Select "Always allow from this computer" and tap OK.

7

Refresh in PeloGo

Return to PeloGo Web and click the "Refresh" button. Your device should now appear in the device selector dropdown.

Common Issues & Solutions

Device not detected in PeloGo

Try using a different USB cable (not all cables support data transfer). Also verify USB Debugging is enabled and the authorization prompt was accepted.

USB Debugging not appearing in Settings

You haven't tapped Build Number 7 times yet. Or you may have tapped too slowly. Try again with quick, successive taps in the About Device screen.
